We used Confidence in Network Meta-Analysis (CINeMA) to assess confidence in treatment effect estimates for the three primary outcomes. Assessments were conducted at the level of treatment comparisons within each connected subnetwork. CINeMA considers six domains: within-study bias (see section 22), reporting bias, indirectness, imprecision, heterogeneity, and incoherence. Each comparison was assigned an overall confidence rating of high, moderate, low, or very low. Domain-level judgements and overall ratings are presented by outcome and subnetwork (see section 5).
