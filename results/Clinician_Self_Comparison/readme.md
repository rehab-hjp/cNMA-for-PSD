Following the approach of Ostinelli et al.,1 we compared treatment effect estimates for the two primary efficacy outcomes, self- and clinician-reported depressive symptom severity at the end of intervention. We first compared SMDs from the two rating methods for matched treatment contrasts within the same trials. For each rating method, we then compared pooled SMDs between trials that reported both types of scores and trials that reported only the rating under analysis.

Reference
1.Ostinelli EG, Schulze M, Zangani C, et al. Comparative efficacy and acceptability of pharmacological, psychological, and neurostimulatory interventions for ADHD in adults: a systematic review and component network meta-analysis. Lancet Psychiatry 2025, 12(1), 32–43.
