For the primary outcome (clinician-rated depressive symptoms [at the end of intervention]), we fitted Bayesian random-effects network meta-regression models within treatment subnetworks to explore potential effect modification. We examined each covariate separately. The covariates were publication year, mean age, the proportion of male participants, the proportions of participants with ischaemic stroke, right-hemisphere stroke, or recurrent stroke, time since stroke, psychiatric comorbidity, baseline depression severity, treatment duration, and drug-dose stability. We centred and scaled continuous covariates before fitting the models. We fitted Bayesian network meta-regression models in R (version 4.4.2) using JAGS through the rjags package. We report posterior estimates with 95% credible intervals. We present results and model-fit summaries by outcome, subnetwork, and covariate.
