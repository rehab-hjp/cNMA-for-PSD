We examined how the additivity assumption affected treatment effect estimates by comparing additive cNMA and standard NMA estimates for the same treatment contrasts. For continuous outcomes, we used equivalence margins of −0.5 and 0.5 SMD units. For binary outcomes, we used margins of 0.5 and 2.0 on the RR scale. These margins defined three regions for assessing agreement between models: left non-equivalence zone (LNEZ), equivalence zone (LEZ and REZ were considered equivalent), right nonequivalence zone (RNEZ).
